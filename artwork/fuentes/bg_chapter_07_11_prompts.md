## Base visual compartida

```text
Use case: stylized-concept for new environments; precise-object-edit or
lighting-weather for architectural and time variants.
Asset type: reusable 2D anime visual-novel background.
Style/medium: polished clean 2D anime background matching the supplied project
references, crisp controlled linework, moderate cel shading, detailed but
orderly environment, grounded natural perspective.
Composition/framing: wide cinematic 16:9; useful open space for character
sprites; keep the lowest 18 percent comparatively simple for the dialogue box.
Constraints: absolutely no people, silhouettes or faces; no readable text,
brands, logos, UI or watermark.
Avoid: photorealism, 3D rendering, fisheye distortion, warped architecture and
excessive bloom.
```
